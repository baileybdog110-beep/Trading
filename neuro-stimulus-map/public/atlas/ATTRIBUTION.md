# Atlas attribution and licences

The meshes in this folder were generated by `scripts/atlas/build_atlas.py` from published
atlas volumes (downloaded from TemplateFlow). They are anatomical / parcellation boundaries
only; they contain no statistical or activation values. Vertex coordinates are MNI
millimetres (RAS+). Input file checksums are recorded in `atlas_manifest.json`.

## cerebra.glb — anatomical regions

- **CerebrA**: Manera AL, Dadar M, Fonov V, Collins DL. *CerebrA, registration and manual
  label correction of Mindboggle-101 atlas for MNI-ICBM152 template.* Scientific Data
  7:237 (2020). doi:10.1038/s41597-020-0557-9. Licensed CC BY 4.0.
- Labels derive from the Mindboggle-101 DKT labelling protocol, which omits the temporal
  pole, frontal pole and banks of the superior temporal sulcus.
- Template: ICBM 2009c nonlinear symmetric (Fonov et al., doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2010.07.033).
  Copyright (C) 1993–2004 Louis Collins, McConnell Brain Imaging Centre, Montreal
  Neurological Institute, McGill University. Permission to use, copy, modify, and
  distribute this software and its documentation for any purpose and without fee is hereby
  granted, provided that the above copyright notice appear in all copies. The authors and
  McGill University make no representations about the suitability of this software for any
  purpose. It is provided "as is" without express or implied warranty.
- Distributed via TemplateFlow (https://www.templateflow.org), `tpl-MNI152NLin2009cSym`.

Build changes: ventricles and the optic chiasm are not rendered; cerebellar grey and white
matter are merged per hemisphere; the three vermis labels are merged; the left/right
brainstem halves are merged. Surfaces were smoothed (Gaussian σ = 0.9 voxel, Taubin) and
decimated, so boundaries are accurate to roughly a millimetre or two, not voxel-exact.

## yeo7.glb — distributed networks

- **Schaefer 2018** 400-parcel parcellation with 7-network assignment: Schaefer A, Kong R,
  Gordon EM, et al. *Local-Global Parcellation of the Human Cerebral Cortex from Intrinsic
  Functional Connectivity MRI.* Cerebral Cortex (2018). MIT licence,
  https://github.com/ThomasYeoLab/CBIG
- Networks from Yeo BTT, Krienen FM, Sepulcre J, et al. *The organization of the human
  cerebral cortex estimated by intrinsic functional connectivity.* J Neurophysiol
  106(3):1125–1165 (2011). doi:10.1152/jn.00338.2011
- Template: ICBM 2009c nonlinear **asymmetric** (TemplateFlow `tpl-MNI152NLin2009cAsym`),
  same licence as above. Because the anatomical meshes use the symmetric version of the
  template, the network overlay is aligned approximately (millimetre scale).
